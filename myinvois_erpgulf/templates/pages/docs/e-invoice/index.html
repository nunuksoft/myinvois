Malaysia e-Invoice Step‑by‑Step Guide

Follow these steps to submit, check status, attach QR, cancel if needed, and use reports.

Step 1: Prepare Invoice Step 2: Submit to LHDN Step 3: Check Status & QR Step 4: Cancel (if needed) Step 5: Find TIN Step 6: Reports & Dashboard Quick Troubleshooting FAQ

Flow Overview

Step 1: Prepare Your Invoice

Make sure the invoice is complete and submitted in ERPNext.
  1. Open the Sales Invoice or Purchase Invoice.
  2. Check that it is submitted (docstatus = 1).
  3. If e‑Invoice buttons are missing, ask your administrator to enable company settings.

Step 2: Submit to LHDN

Sales Invoice

  1. Open the Sales Invoice.
  2. Click “Submit Invoice To LHDN”.
  3. Wait for the confirmation message.
  4. Reload the invoice to see updated fields.

Purchase Invoice

  1. Open the Purchase Invoice.
  2. Click “Submit Invoice to LHDN”.
  3. Wait for the confirmation message.
  4. Reload the invoice to see updated fields.

Step 3: Check Status and QR

  1. Open the submitted invoice.
  2. Click “Get Status of SubmittedDoc”.
  3. When status becomes “VALID”, a QR code is attached automatically.
  4. If QR is missing, click status again or reload the invoice.

Step 4: Cancel (if needed)

Cancellations are only allowed within 72 hours of submission.
  1. Open the submitted invoice you need to cancel.
  2. Cancel the document inside ERPNext.
  3. The system sends a cancellation request to LHDN.
  4. Verify the status on the invoice after cancellation.

Step 5: Find Customer/Supplier TIN

Customer TIN (Sales)

  1. Open the Sales Invoice.
  2. Click “Check Customer TIN”.
  3. When found, the TIN fills the customer’s TIN field.

Supplier TIN (Purchase)

  1. Open the Purchase Invoice.
  2. Click “Check Supplier TIN”.
  3. When found, the TIN fills the supplier’s TIN field.

Step 6: Use Reports and Dashboard

  1. Open the app’s Reports to view Sales Status, Purchase Status, and VAT reports.
  2. Use the LHDN Dashboard for real‑time submission insights.

Quick Troubleshooting

  • Submit button missing: ensure the invoice is submitted; ask admin to enable e‑Invoice settings.
  • Status pending: click “Get Status of SubmittedDoc” again later.
  • QR missing: once status is “VALID”, reload; if still missing, click status again.
  • Cancellation denied: you may be past the 72‑hour window.
  • TIN not found: confirm the name or ID; try again.

FAQ

  • What happens after I submit? The system sends your invoice to LHDN and stores the response.
  • When do I get a QR? When LHDN marks the document as “VALID”.
  • Can I cancel any time? No, only within 72 hours after submission.
  • Who configures the app? Your administrator handles company settings and access.
